Privacy Policy

Effective Date: May 2026

Kerala Solar Prosumer Bill Calculator (KSPC) (“the App”) is an independent utility application developed and maintained by anwarbio for estimating electricity bills, solar net metering calculations, and managing meter records for Kerala electricity consumers. This page explains the policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal information.

This app is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or officially connected to Kerala State Electricity Board (KSEB) or any government organization. Billing calculations are estimates based on publicly available tariff information.

Information Collection and Use

The App does not collect, store, or share any personally identifiable information. All data (such as meter readings, bill history, or backups) are stored locally on your device. If you choose to use the Google Drive backup feature, the data is stored in your own Google Drive account and never sent to any external server.

Google Services

The App may use third-party services such as Google Drive API to allow you to back up and restore your own data. These services are governed by their own privacy policies:

Security

The App does not transmit your data to any external server controlled by the developer. All backups are under your control in your Google Drive.
